# ORBIT COMMANDO '94 — Game Design

A 16-bit-style run & gun where the ground is round. You run around small
planetoids, jump across gravity fields, ride vehicles and clear three worlds
of a comically bureaucratic alien empire (the Glorbo). Tone: loud, silly and
action-packed; PEGI 16 cartoon violence, no gore.

## 1. Pillars

1. **Gravity is the level design.** Every level mixes flat ground with
   uniform gravity, zero-g areas and round planetoids with radial gravity.
   Jumping from one field to another is the core skill.
2. **Arcade honesty.** Deterministic 60 Hz simulation and readable enemy
   tells. A good player can finish on one credit. The game never sells
   power in ranked modes.
3. **The 90s, lovingly.** Attract mode, CRT filter, secret codes, a
   juke-box, a gallery, cassette collectibles and an "insert coin" countdown.

## 2. Controls

| Action | Touch (default layout) | Keyboard / pad |
|---|---|---|
| Move / aim (8 directions) | left virtual stick | arrows / D-pad |
| Jump (hold for higher) | A | Z / A |
| Fire (hold = auto) | B | X / B |
| Grenade | G | C / Y |
| Dash (campaign: from level 1-3; always in the other modes) | DASH button | V / RB |
| Pause | top-right button | Esc / Start |

- **Camera:** "rotating" (the default) keeps the hero's feet down on
  planetoids. "Fixed" keeps the world still and helps people prone to
  motion sickness.
- **Touch layout:** editable in Settings → Controls (position, size,
  opacity), with optional vibration.

## 3. Movement and physics

- **Hero shape:** a capsule body moving in its local frame (right/up)
  relative to the current gravity field. The nearest surface wins, sampled
  at the feet.
- **Movement numbers:**
  - jump apex about 88 px
  - gaps between feet at most 80 px
  - planetoid surfaces at most 140 px apart
  - a planet's influence reaches radius + 80
- **Field changes:** on an abrupt change (up vectors more than ~18°
  apart), world velocity is kept, so jumps don't reverse.
- **Platforms:** one-way platforms; vehicles have their own capsules and
  fall-out death.

All numbers live in `assets/data/balance.json` (mirrored by `Balance` in
`lib/game/sim/balance.dart`) and can be tuned without touching code.

## 4. Content

### Worlds and levels (15)

| # | World 1 — Quiet Asteroid Belt | World 2 — Glorbo Space Station | World 3 — Glorbo Homeworld |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cintura Tranquilla | Corridoio Margherita | Arcipelago Glorbo |
| 2 | Campo di Sassi | Sala Antigravità | Navetta nel Vuoto (shuttle) |
| 3 | Il Mech a Molla (mech) | Moto-Razzo Express (rocket bike) | Fabbrica di Moduli |
| 4 | Lato Oscuro del Sasso | Anelli della Pizza | Ultimo Sportello |
| 5 | **Boss: Timbratore Supremo** | **Boss: La Grande Lavatrice Cosmica** | **Boss: Imperatore Glorbo** |

- **Level files:** Tiled JSON (`assets/levels/*.tmj`), generated by
  `tools/level_gen` and checked by `tools/level_validation/validate.py`
  (reachability, gap sizes, spawn safety).
- **Beatability:** the autopilot bot finishes every level in the test
  suite.
- **Contents of every level:**
  - 3 hostages (bonus points and a medal)
  - 1 hidden cassette (unlocks a juke-box track)
  - checkpoints
  - a par time

### Heroes (4)
- Roxy (fast) and Brutus (big gun) are available from the start.
- Nyx unlocks after World 2.
- Doc unlocks after the campaign.

Heroes are cosmetic plus a small trait; balance is shared.

### Weapons (7)

| Weapon | Role |
|---|---|
| Blaster | Infinite default; fast, low damage |
| Spread | 5-pellet fan, crowd control |
| Laser | Continuous beam, pierces |
| Plasma | Slow heavy orb, splash |
| Homing | Seeks the nearest target |
| Flame | Short range, very high DPS |
| Ricochet | Bounces off surfaces, great around planetoids |

- Every weapon except the Blaster comes from a pickup and has limited ammo.
- Grenades follow gravity, so they arc around planets.

### Enemies (12)

| Group | Enemies |
|---|---|
| Troopers | Grunt (rifle), Bulwark (shield + gatling), Sniper (visible laser sight), Clerk (throws stamped forms) |
| Flyers | Rocket (hovering rocketeer), Drone (bomber), Jelly (electric pulse), Swarm (metal insects) |
| Heavies | Cyclops (minigun walker), Minimech (jumper), Turret (crawls on curved surfaces), Roller (kamikaze) |

Every attack has a tell of at least 20 ticks, and its colour depends on the
attack.

### Bosses (3)

| Boss | Fight | Weak point |
|---|---|---|
| Stamper | Slams the planet, sending shockwaves you jump over; stamps fall in arcs | Ink tank on its back (exposed after a slam) |
| Washer | Spin cycle pulls you in, then flings soap bubbles | Control panel that opens in "rinse" phases |
| Emperor Glorbo | Three phases: throne lasers, form storm, final "planet scale" in zero-g | Crown jewel |

Boss arenas clamp the camera; HP and phase thresholds live in the balance
file.

### Vehicles (3)

| Vehicle | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Spring Mech | Big jump, stomp, cannon |
| Rocket Bike | Fast, follows planet curvature, ramps |
| Shuttle | Free flight in zero-g, twin guns |

- A vehicle absorbs hits, then ejects you.
- A destroyed vehicle respawns at the checkpoint.

## 5. Scoring and ranks

```
total = score + time bonus (under par) + no-death bonus
rank  = S / A / B / C / D thresholds per level (rankFor in game_sim.dart)
```

- **Medals:** no deaths, all hostages, speedrun (under par).
- **Rewards:** tokens (soft currency), pilot XP and campaign credits.

## 6. Modes

| Mode | Unlock | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign | start | 15 levels in order; continues with credits; Workshop upgrades apply (not on Arcade difficulty) |
| Arcade | finish World 1 | all levels in a row on limited credits, high-score table with initials |
| Boss Rush | finish the campaign | the 3 bosses back to back, timed |
| Weekly Challenge | start | one level plus a modifier ("Ricochet only", "Low gravity", …), fixed seed; race your best ghost |

Arcade, Boss Rush and Weekly are ranked: paid upgrades are off
(`ranked_allow_paid_upgrades = false`).

## 7. Difficulty

| Difficulty | Settings |
|---|---|
| Easy | 3 HP, aim assist, slower enemies |
| Normal | 1 HP, the classic one-hit arcade rule (Workshop HP upgrades add more) |
| Arcade | 1 HP, upgrades disabled |

## 8. The 90s layer

- **Title screen:** attract mode alternates, every 22 s, between the
  high-score table and a demo run played by the autopilot.
- **CRT filter:** three presets (arcade, TV, "broken tube") in a GLSL
  shader; off by default.
- **Secret codes** (swipes on the title screen, or arrows/D-pad):
  - two clockwise orbits (↑→↓← ×2) → big heads
  - ↓↓↓↑↑↑ → giant pixels
  - ←→←→↓↑ → broken tube

  They are original sequences, chosen to be unlike any famous code.
- **Collection screens:**
  - juke-box: every music track, unlocked by cassettes
  - gallery: enemies, bosses and concept sheets
  - trophies: 32 in-game achievements
- **Continue screen:** a 9-second countdown with "INSERT COIN" blink and
  game-over jingle.

## 9. Meta

- **Workshop:** permanent upgrades (damage, extra HP, starting shield,
  fire rate, starting grenades) bought with tokens.
  - Gems can also be used, at a fixed exchange shown in euro.
  - Capsule fragments (10 = one level) are another path.
- **Daily login calendar:** 7 days, with free rewards only.
- **Season Pass:** 30 tiers from XP, with a free and a premium track
  (cosmetics and soft currency only).

## 10. Audio

- **Generator:** all sound is generated by `tools/audio/` (a 4-channel
  chip synth and tracker).
- **Contents:** 33 SFX, 6 music tracks (title, three worlds, boss,
  victory) and 6 jingles.
- **Mixing:** music and SFX volumes are separate; the music ducks on
  jingles.
