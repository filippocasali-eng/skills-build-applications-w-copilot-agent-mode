Blockitty: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Blockitty ("the game") is a casual puzzle game. This policy explains what data is processed when you play.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (scores, collection, settings, optional player name) is stored only on your device. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect this data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve, measure and personalize ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent via Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Purchasing "No Ads" removes interstitial ads; optional rewarded videos remain available only if you choose to watch them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google provides us with a purchase confirmation so we can deliver your items.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device. No data is sent anywhere to do this.

Sharing

When you share a score or challenge link, you choose the app and the recipients. The link contains your score, a game seed and your optional player name.

Children

Blockitty is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can reset your ad ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings, deny notifications, and delete all game data by using "Reset progress" in Settings or uninstalling the app.
